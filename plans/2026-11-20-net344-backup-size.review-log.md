All 9 findings taken. I redesigned the fitting step (§2.3, §10).

Findings #2, #3, #4 and #7 all came from per-entry acknowledgement trimming, the 413 refusal and the suppression caps.

**New rule.** A restore keeps the box's own copy of any section a backup leaves out. So the export leaves out whole sections in a fixed order until the file fits: acknowledgements first, then the largest of suppressions, excluded IPs and pictures. The note says what was left out at download, and the warning says it again at restore.

**How the rule works.**
- Sizes are measured by streaming, so the history is never built as one string.
- The escape for lone surrogates acts per character, so section sizes add up.

**What goes.** No caps, no 413 at export, and no recovery procedures.
